Your count, on the home screen.

Two widgets: one to switch Tally on or off, and one to show today's time.

The eye widget.

It shows today's total swipes and an eye switch. Tap the eye to pause or resume counting. Tap anywhere else to open Tally.

When it is off the number dims, the label says paused, and the count is kept. Swipes are not counted while it is off.

It comes in a square 2x2 and a small 2x1. Larger sizes stretch.

The time widget.

It shows today's time as the big number, with one plain line under it about what that time could be.

Under 10 minutes it shows no line. The activities are rough round numbers, not measurements.

Small on purpose.

The daily total resets at midnight on your phone's clock. There is no yesterday, no best day, no streak and no chart. It is stored only on your phone.

The widgets are part of the prototype. Details may change before release.
