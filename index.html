See how much you scroll.

Tally is an Android app. It counts your swipes and minutes in the apps you pick, puts the total in the middle of your screen, and slowly dims the screen as the count climbs.

Android only for now. Drag the phone below to try it.

What Tally leaves alone.

  • Your apps. Tally never blocks an app or locks your phone. Touches pass through to the app underneath.
  • Your content. It reads no text, messages or posts from the apps it counts. The counts stay on your phone.
  • Your time. No time limits, no accounts, no streaks, no scores and no history.
  • Your choice. You pick the apps, the style, the veil speed and the hours.

Swipe up

A number in the middle of the screen.

Pick the apps you scroll. Tally counts swipes and minutes in them and shows the total over the app. The number turns from white to amber to red as it climbs.

The screen gets harder to see.

With every swipe the edges close in a little more, until only a small clear patch is left. You set how fast. It never covers the middle completely, and touches still reach the app.

SlowFast

Tally sits on top of your apps.

You do not open Tally to use it. Open the apps you scroll, like a video or photo app, and Tally is already there. It puts a number in the middle of the screen: how many times you have swiped. The more you swipe, the bigger the number gets.

The app underneath works like normal. You can still tap, scroll and type, and Tally never gets in the way. It only shows on the apps you pick. It stays off your phone calls and your phone's own screens.

As the number climbs, Tally also slowly darkens the edges of the screen. Nothing is blocked. It just gets harder to keep going.

  • The number. Counts your swipes.
  • The dark edges. Close in as you scroll.
  • The video. The app underneath.

On your home screen too.

Three widgets. The eye widget shows today's swipes and an eye to switch Tally on or off. The time widget shows today's minutes with one plain line about what that time could be. The money widget shows that time as euros at minimum wage, gross.

Tap the eye to pause counting. Tap anywhere else to open Tally. When it is off, the number dims and the label says paused.

These widgets are live. Use the eye, or swipe in the demo. The daily total resets at midnight on your phone's clock, with no history. Widgets are part of the prototype and may change.

You pick the apps.

Tally finds the common social apps on your phone and turns them on for you. Add any other app, and switch off the ones you do not want counted. Browsers start switched off, because Tally cannot tell which site you are on.

To show a number over other apps, Android asks you to turn on Tally's accessibility setting yourself. Tally uses it only to count your swipes and the time an app is open. It reads no text, messages or posts.
