Tally counts how much you scroll.

It puts a number on your screen while you scroll a feed app, and the number gets louder as it climbs. It never blocks or locks anything.

What it does

Pick the apps you scroll. Tally counts your swipes and minutes in them and shows the total in the middle of the screen. Touches still reach the app underneath.

Turn the number on all day, or only during a time window you set, such as 21:00 to 06:00.

Nothing is blocked. There are no time limits, no lock screens and no accounts. Tally reads no text or content from the apps it counts, and the counts stay on your phone.

Tally is Android only for now.
